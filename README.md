# 📊 Business Data Management – Capstone Project  
## Product Portfolio Optimization & Customer Intelligence  

---

## 🧾 Project Overview  
This project was completed as part of the **Business Data Management (BDM) Capstone**, an independent applied analytics study designed to solve real-world business problems using data.

**Objective of the BDM Capstone:**  
Identify operational/business challenges faced by an organization, collect primary data, perform data cleaning and transformation, conduct statistical and analytical modeling, and deliver actionable insights for decision-making.

---

## 🏢 Industry Context  
The study was conducted for a **B2B Scientific Laboratory Equipment Distributor** (*Sigma Scientific Products*).

### **Business Challenges Identified**

### 🔹 Product Rationalization  
Rapid growth of SKUs leading to:
- Portfolio complexity  
- Pricing inconsistencies  
- Inventory inefficiencies  

### 🔹 Customer & Sales Intelligence Gap  
Lack of structured understanding of:
- Customer purchasing behaviour  
- High-value vs low-value customers  
- Sales seasonality and geography  

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives  
- Analyze product portfolio diversity and redundancy  
- Evaluate pricing dispersion and demand patterns  
- Identify high-value / low-value SKUs  
- Segment customers using ML techniques  
- Identify churn-risk customers  
- Study sales seasonality & forecasting patterns  
- Evaluate geographical sales concentration  

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Cleaning  
Performed extensive preprocessing on:

✔ **Sales Transactions Dataset**  
✔ **Product Master Dataset**

### **Key Steps**
- Missing value treatment  
- Column normalization  
- Date parsing  
- Outlier identification  
- Feature engineering  
- Customer & SKU aggregation  

---

## 📈 Analytical Methods Applied  

### 🔹 1. Product Portfolio Analysis  
- Category distribution analysis  
- Price dispersion (Box Plot)  
- Price vs Quantity correlation  
- Pareto (High-Value vs Low-Value Products)  

---

### 🔹 2. Comparative Pricing & Sales Intelligence  
- Quartile & IQR analysis  
- Identification of pricing outliers  
- Demand-price behaviour analysis  

---

### 🔹 3. Customer Segmentation  

Applied **unsupervised learning techniques:**

**K-Means Clustering**  
Segmented customers based on:
- Total sales value  
- Purchase frequency  
- Order volume  
- Average order value  

**RFM Analysis**  
Identified:
- Loyal customers  
- Churn-risk customers  
- Low-engagement customers  

---

### 🔹 4. Time-Series Analysis  
- Monthly sales trend  
- Seasonality detection  
- Peak & dip identification  

---

### 🔹 5. Geographical Sales Analysis  
- Regional revenue concentration  
- Heatmap visualization  
- Market expansion insights  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights Generated  

✔ Revenue highly concentrated in few SKUs  
✔ Significant price dispersion across products  
✔ Negative Price–Demand relationship  
✔ Clear customer behavioural clusters  
✔ High churn-risk customer segment identified  
✔ Seasonal demand peaks (Feb–Mar) detected  
✔ Strong sales concentration in Southern states  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations  
- SKU rationalization & overlap reduction  
- Inventory policy by SKU class  
- Strategic bundling of fast movers  
- Margin-aware pricing adjustments  
- Account-based strategies for high-value customers  
- Reactivation campaigns for low-RFM customers  
- Region-focused sales & stocking strategy  

---

## 🚀 Business Impact  

✔ Reduced inventory cost & capital lock-in risk  
✔ Improved revenue optimization opportunities  
✔ Enhanced customer targeting & retention strategy
